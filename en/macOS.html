Open Terminal (no admin password needed) and run:


curl -fsSL https://lomosw.lomorage.com/mac/install.sh | bash

This installs "Lomorage" to your user profile and starts it automatically -- no .dmg, no reboot. A "Lomorage" icon appears in your menu bar and in ~/Applications (findable via Spotlight/Launchpad); open it from either any time.

See the full install guide for details -- choosing a custom data folder, accessing it from your phone, uninstalling, and more.

Alternative: prefer a traditional installer with a GUI setup wizard instead? Download the DMG installer.
